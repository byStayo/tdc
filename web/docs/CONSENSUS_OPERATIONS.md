## Promotion and rollback

The Fed S124.8.t addition is quarterly private-credit context, not daily private-equity flows or a directional input. Three distinct measures retain $bn SAAR units: loan net acquisition, all financial-asset net acquisition (which includes loans), and net fund share issues. The September 11, 2026 vintage has source inputs through 2026 Q1; Q2 is estimated. The dated table URL/hash and reviewed PDF hash are saved in the signal. A changed release fails closed until units, latest period and input lag are reviewed; only a transport outage may retain earlier evidence, with original timestamps and normal age limits. The private-capital panel preserves older SEC asset stocks separately. No historical edition receives this source retroactively.

The subscription publisher maintains `lib/consensus/reviewed-developments.json` after reading actual primary releases. Each source keeps its URL, original publication date (date-only where no time is supplied), actual retrieval time and SHA256 of the privately retrieved document. Save short factual paraphrases and separately labelled instrument-specific transmission channels, with explicit unknowns. Never infer a market reaction or analyst beat from positive company growth. Keep source documents private; do not copy full releases. A future timestamp, unapproved source host, missing citation, mismatched company or conflicting same-quarter revenue blocks publication.

The morning view displays up to three scoped developments. News reviews carry no directional score, avoiding an extra vote for the same earnings or rates fact. Seven-day freshness controls coverage; older reviews remain dated context. A newer company quarterly statement may supersede an older SEC revenue observation and add GAAP EPS. Its chart contains only the current and comparable year-earlier quarter. A later-period SEC filing supersedes that saved release; a conflicting same-period value requires factual review. Review timestamps alone do not create an edition. Historical snapshots retain their original evidence and are never enriched retroactively.

Prospective models are separate for each supported instrument and horizon. Scoring and training use `last-edition-before-entry-open-v1` to select one edition per instrument, horizon and entry session by publication time. The private outcome journal preserves earlier rows and links them to its completed transaction. The public `outcomes.json` has the edition, instrument, horizon and rule version for each resolution. `outcome-selection.json` identifies superseded editions. The market view summarizes the five instrument cohorts for the selected horizon.

The `prospective-walk-forward-2` gate requires at least500 resolved observations,50 training rows with both labels,20 validation rows and100 new non-overlapping test windows. Initial60/20/20 chronological boundaries purge labels crossing split boundaries. Later test windows must begin after the last consumed label resolves, even when no incumbent was promoted. Greedy chronological selection excludes overlapping weekly/monthly label windows. At least20 newly resolved labels are required on every attempt. Longer horizons therefore need substantially more than500 raw observations. Normalization uses training only; four regularization choices compete on validation only. Historical FX experiments remain separate and cannot activate these models.

Before fitting/evaluating, the locked evaluator appends and fsyncs an immutable `public/consensus/evaluations/<instrument>-<horizon>-<attempt>.json` reservation. It records test IDs/windows, dataset hash and `consumedThrough`, with no raw prices. Failure or interruption still consumes that window; never delete or reset it. Missing reservation history for an already evaluated candidate fails closed. A legacy untracked evaluated artifact requires explicit audit migration; none existed when this policy was introduced. Do not rerun or relabel the nine saved historical FX experiments to satisfy this gate.

Promotion must improve Brier by more than0.01 against the training base rate,50/50 and any incumbent, with a positive basic-bootstrap95% lower bound and a one-sided paired-loss test below the declared alpha. Circular blocks use length`ceil(cuberoot(n))`,at least19,999resamples and a fixed dataset-derived seed. Bonferroni across15cohorts plus attempt budget`0.05/(15*n*(n+1))` is deliberately more conservative than a batch Holm gate and also limits repeated attempts. Bootstrap evidence is approximate under serial dependence; non-overlap does not establish independence. Resampling resolution grows to max(19,999, ceil(4/alpha)-1) with a cap of1,999,999 resamples; exceeding the cap stops before a test is inspected. Attempt8 can therefore qualify rather than being impossible by construction. Do not retry the same holdout with tuned settings. A methodology revision needs a fresh future test window.

Method references: [scikit-learn chronological evaluation and test-set leakage](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html), [SciPy paired/basic bootstrap definitions](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.bootstrap.html), and [arch circular block bootstrap](https://arch.readthedocs.io/en/latest/bootstrap/generated/arch.bootstrap.CircularBlockBootstrap.html). These describe the methods, not the product's predictive performance. The thresholds and alpha schedule above are declared product policies.

The evaluator archives candidate and active-model artifacts and updates the registry only when gates pass. Prebuild and publication validate active promotion evidence against its durable reservation. Reports distinguish the current candidate's promotion from an already active incumbent. Future snapshots freeze model ID, training time and probability. Historical outcomes retain their original model and price basis. To roll back, select a previously archived compatible active model, record the reason and change the registry under the shared publication lock; do not rewrite old snapshots, outcomes or consumed-test reservations.

## Current verification boundary

### As-issued history

New publications freeze all six instruments and three horizons in `snapshot.consensus` before hashing. The API, morning verdict, outcome evaluator and training features read those saved results. Existing archives remain byte-for-byte unchanged: versions `consensus-rules-1.0.0` and `1.1.0` are interpreted by the frozen `engines/v1.ts` implementation using their original cutoff. Never edit that historical engine or silently route an unknown legacy version to the active engine. Add an explicit version implementation when methodology changes.

Archive verification checks saved structure, provenance, chronology and hashes; it does not regenerate past wording or derived review/holdings signals with current code. New candidate publications still cross-check every derivation, including stored consensus. Latest source panels may mark observations stale as time passes; the saved verdict and its publication coverage remain unchanged and the page discloses observations that aged out. An abstaining verdict has no directional needle and lists the actual engine reasons.

The November 3 holdings increment has 78 tracked inputs: 65 current observations, one stale SEC private-equity aggregate and 12 missing inputs. Weighted requested coverage is about55%; the consensus abstains. Cross-border Treasury flows, GDELT financial-news tone, three reviewed primary developments and BLS/FOMC/Treasury/OCC/BoE/ECB/BoJ calendars are connected. Missing US prices/options, social/retail, analyst estimates, off-exchange and actual private-equity investment flows remain material gaps. FINRA private integration is tested with fixtures but awaits the user's free Public credential; it is not live coverage. Nine real ECB currency-history candidates were evaluated; none passed the declared improvement threshold. No prospective outcome or active model is claimed.

After a provider failure, the collector can retain a previously verified numeric series or news batch. It preserves the observation/retrieval dates, source hash and methodology, adds a visible refresh notice, and retains the collection error. Existing stale limits continue to apply. Calendar events are not revived through this fallback. A failed refresh alone cannot create a new edition. Outcome evidence requires canonical UTC timestamps aligned with the named New York session date; mixed-offset string comparisons cannot silently assign a wrong outcome.
