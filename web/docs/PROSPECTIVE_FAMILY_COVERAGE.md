## Treasury dealer study

From `web`, run `npm run consensus:dealer-study -- --register` once before a future source receipt. The registration is idempotent. `npm run consensus:dealer-study -- --status` reads private registration and enrollment counts. The ordinary full source preparation records the first observed weekly originals. The normal pre-open verifier enrolls the TLT decision, and the production evaluator sends its first supported settlement through the shared private study mechanism.

Candidate and control are the reported weekly changes for nominal Treasury coupons maturing in over 21 years and over 11 through 21 years, respectively. The registered hypothesis expects a negative relation between dealer long-duration inventory growth and the next eligible TLT session. Each change must equal the difference between two separately retained weekly balances. The issuer’s additions and removals list must verify an unchanged reporting panel. The data and roster come from the Federal Reserve Bank of New York. The Daily Consensus calculates the signed predictor and performs the research.
